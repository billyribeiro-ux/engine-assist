# Foreman components (Stage 4.5)

Design system for Foreman v1. Tokens: `docs/design/tokens.css`. Contrast proof: `docs/design/contrast.md` (generated by `contrast-check.py`). Mockups: `docs/design/mockups/*.html`, built from `docs/design/_build/` with `python3 docs/design/_build/build.py`; screenshots with `_build/shoot.py`.

v1.1 screens (tickets and the Crew Loop, lessons view, change requests) are out of scope for these drawings.

## Principles

- **One bold element.** The sheet strip is the only saturated, decorated surface (cyanotype, blueprint grid, phase edges, stamps). Everything else is native-quiet: SF Pro, hairline rules, square sheet edges, 6 px radius on controls only.
- **One orchestrated motion.** The approval stamp (220 ms scale-and-settle). Other motion only answers an action: drawer 160 ms, inspector 140 ms, toggles 100 ms. All of it is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Status is shape plus color.** Every status has a glyph and words: check (done, pass), cross (fail, invalid), filled dot (running, in progress), open ring (ready, not started), magnifier (inspecting), stamp (signed), hatched square (not applicable), dashed square (needs annotation), question mark (undecided), lock (core, locked), clock (waiting). `--attention` appears only for things waiting on you.
- **Writing.** Sentence case, plain verbs, the same verb through a flow ("Sign packet" → "Packet signed"). Errors say what happened and how to fix it. Not-applicable and locked states say why in one line.
- **Phase marks in mockups.** A dashed `P2` tag marks elements that ship in phase 2; untagged elements are P1. The dashed box at the bottom left of each mockup is the mockup legend and theme toggle, not part of the app.

## Sheet strip (F-030, P2)

**Anatomy.** Cyanotype band with a 12 px blueprint grid (`--strip-grid`). 19 sheets in six phase groups (Pre-construction 0–4, Structure 5–8, Systems 9–11, Finishes 12–15, Occupancy 16–17, Ownership 18). Each sheet: a 1 px linework outline, the number bottom-left in the drafting face, a status mark top-right, a 3 px phase edge along the bottom (`--phase-*`, tints that reach 3:1 on cyanotype). Under each group, a dimension line with end ticks and the phase name. The open sheet is inverted (linework fill, cyanotype number) with a section arrow pointing down at the sheet below.

**States.**

| State | Outline | Mark | Spoken |
|---|---|---|---|
| Locked | `--strip-muted` | none | "Stage 3, Permits & Zoning: locked (until Stage 2 is signed)" |
| Ready | linework | open ring | "…: ready" |
| In progress | inverted | filled dot | "…: in progress" |
| Inspecting | linework or inverted | magnifier | "…: inspecting" |
| Waiting for signature | linework | magnifier in `--strip-attention` | "…: packet waiting for your signature" |
| Signed | linework | stamp (rotated −14°) | "…: signed" |
| Not applicable | `--strip-muted`, hatched fill | none | "…: not applicable (idea_known = yes)" |
| Needs annotation | dashed `--strip-muted` | none | "…: needs annotation" |
| Undecided | dashed | question mark | "…: a condition is undecided (payments = undecided)" |
| Hover | 8% linework wash; native tooltip with number, name and status | | |
| Focus | 2 px `--focus-on-strip` ring, 2 px offset | | |

**The stamp (the one orchestrated motion).** After signing passes read-back (F-014), the signed sheet's stamp scales from 1.9 to 0.9 to 1 with a small overshoot (`--ease-stamp`, `--dur-stamp` 220 ms, 300 ms after the dialog closes so the eye is back on the strip), and the next applicable sheet changes to ready. Under Reduce Motion the stamp appears without movement. Demo: `gate.html#signed`.

**Keyboard.** The strip is one tab stop (roving tabindex): ← → move between sheets, Home / End jump to 0 / 18, Return opens the stage. ⌘] / ⌘[ next / previous stage from anywhere, ⌘G then a number to jump.

**Accessibility.** `<nav aria-label="TradeLog drawing set: 19 stages">` containing one `<ol>` per phase group labelled "Pre-construction, stages 0 to 4". Each sheet is a link with a full `aria-label` (number, name, status, reason); the open sheet has `aria-current="page"`. Marks and dimension lines are `aria-hidden`. Increase Contrast swaps hatching and dashes for solid outlines.

**Miniature strip** (Today, P2): the same states at 6 × 14 px, `role="img"` with a one-sentence label ("Stage 2 of 19 in progress; stage 1 signed").

## Title block (F-030, P2)

Drafting box at the right of the stage header, in the drafting face. Cells: Sheet (large number, "of 19"), Project, Revision (blueprint version and lock commit), DRI, Status ("In progress, 4 of 9" or "Signed 2026-10-19"). 1.5 px `--ink-muted` frame, hairline `--rule` cell lines, 11 px labels. Values truncate with an ellipsis and a tooltip; the full text is in the accessible name.

**States:** in progress, inspecting, signed (Status cell shows the date and a stamp glyph), signed on another branch ("Signed on handoff/stage-05, not merged"). **Accessibility:** `role="group" aria-label="Title block"`; label/value pairs read in order. Not focusable.

## Step (callout row) (F-007, F-030)

Steps are callouts on the sheet, not cards. Grid: a 64 px margin holding a circular callout with the step number (it's a real sequence) and a short leader line, then the title row (title, state, lock), the spec line, and, for the selected step, the step body. Hairline rule between steps; no backgrounds except the selected step (`--sheet-raised`).

**States:** not started (ring), ready, copied (chat: "Copied 6 min ago, waiting for the packet", attention), running (dot, accent, names the terminal tab), done (check, pass; spec line hidden to keep done rows short), skipped (skip glyph and the reason), not applicable (see below), waiting until a date (clock), undecided ("Decide payments to continue"), needs annotation (dashed callout, "Not runnable: needs annotation"), added for this project (badge; always confirms before running, SR-07), selected (raised, accent callout).

**Keyboard.** J / K or ↓ / ↑ move the selection; Return expands; ⌘↵ runs the selected step; ⌘⇧C copy prompt; ⌘⌥C copy command; ⌘D marks a chat step done; ⌘⇧V pastes back into it.

**Accessibility.** The steps are an `<ol aria-label="Steps in Stage 2">`; each title is an `<h2>` whose button has `aria-expanded`; the selected step has `aria-current="step"`. The callout number is `aria-hidden` (the title row carries it). State text is real text, never color alone.

## Spec line (F-005)

The run config as a small schedule: surface (icon + "CLI in spec/", "CLI, continues 2.2", "Chat, same chat as 1.1"), model, effort, mode, agents, then a note. Segments separated by hairline rules, not dots. Model in `--ink` medium; other values `--ink-muted`.

**States:** template value; substituted or overridden (dotted underline in `--ink`; hover or focus shows the source: "Fable 5.1, high (Max) → Opus 5.5, xhigh (substituted)"); condition-dropped agent ("market-analyst not applicable").

**Accessibility.** `<dl aria-label="Runs with">` with visually hidden `<dt>` labels (Surface, Model, Effort, Mode, Agents), so VoiceOver reads "Model, Fable 5.1". The explain-on-hover popover is also reachable by focus (each edited value is a button with `aria-describedby`).

## Prompt block (F-006)

Mono 12.5 / 1.5 on `--well`, ≤ 88 ch, read-only, wrapped. Shows the first six lines with "Show all 21 lines" (`aria-expanded`). Resolved variables: `--accent-ink` with a dotted underline; tooltip and accessible description say where the value came from ("APP_NAME from project.json"). Unresolved required tokens: attention pills (buttons) that focus their input; Run stays disabled until none are left. Optional parts are outlined with a dashed `--control-edge` box. Remapped packet paths show a note under the caption. Above the prompt sits the action row (Appendix A.11: prompt and argv are one click away under the primary action).

**Accessibility.** `<pre aria-label="Resolved prompt for 2.4">`; pills are `<button>`s named "Fill Validation results". Copying puts the resolved text on the clipboard and announces "Prompt copied" through the toast region.

## Run-input form (F-006)

Fields from the step's `in=` annotations, above the prompt: label, then a text input or multiline textarea; "(run input, remembered for TradeLog)" when `remember` is set. Over 64 KB: warning "Chat paste limits may apply". **States:** empty (Run disabled, reason names the field), filled, remembered, error. **Keyboard:** Tab order follows the form; ⌘↵ inside a textarea runs the step. **Accessibility:** `<label for>` on every field; errors via `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`.

## Optional-part toggle (F-006)

A switch under the run inputs: "Include optional part: launch-day press release and FAQ". Default on. Off removes the text from the prompt and records it under SKIPPED at signing. **States:** on (`--accent-ink` track), off (`--control-edge` knob), disabled after the step ran (the choice is recorded). **Keyboard:** Space toggles. **Accessibility:** `role="switch" aria-checked`, the label names the part. The track change animates over 100 ms only when motion is allowed.

## Step actions (F-008, F-012, F-024)

One primary button that matches the surface: "Run in terminal ⌘↵" (CLI), "Copy and open ⌘↵" (chat), "Open in Desktop" (Desktop). Secondary: Copy prompt, Copy command, Skip (only for optional steps; asks for a one-line reason), and "Show the exact command" (a disclosure with the full argv: the SR-03 preview). When Run is disabled, the reason sits next to it in words ("Waits for 2.3: Claude is still working in this session. Run re-arms it with Accept edits when it's idle."). A confirmation dialog appears only for an added step, a mode different from the template's, the first launch in a project, or `auto`/`dontAsk`.

**Continuing steps (re-arm, F-010).** The reason line says Run re-arms the session. In P1, Run first asks Billy to confirm "Claude is idle and nothing is running in the background" (the button says what it asserts). After re-arm, the terminal shows a divider: `── 2.3 Feature specifications · Fable 5.1 · high · Accept edits · permission grants reset ──`.

**Broken chain.** "This conversation no longer exists (Claude Code removes transcripts after cleanupPeriodDays)" with the action **Start a fresh chain with context** (Today and Run log mockups).

**Accessibility.** Disabled buttons keep focusability through `aria-disabled` plus `aria-describedby` pointing at the reason, so VoiceOver reads why.

## Chat setup chips and paste-back (F-012)

**Chips:** checkable reminders ("Same chat as 1.1" in bold, model, effort, Web search, Research) with a note that Foreman can't set them in claude.ai. `role="checkbox"` each, Space toggles; they don't block anything. **Chat link:** the stored conversation URL as a link button; "Link this chat" when missing.

**Paste-back (⌘⇧V):** a region on the step. Empty: dashed outline "Paste the packet back (⌘⇧V)". Found: solid `--pass` outline, "Packet found on the clipboard: Stage 1 → Stage 2", the exact writes (Create / Overwrite with diff / Normalized), and "Write 3 files" / Cancel. Errors in place: "Clipboard is empty", "This is the Stage 3 packet; this step expects Stage 2", "Paste one packet at a time". `role="region"` with `aria-live="polite"` so the result is announced.

## Insert point (+) (F-025, P2)

An 18 px circular button centred in the step margin, on the rule between two steps. Opens a menu: Add prompt, Add inspection item (both "this project only"). **States:** rest (muted), hover/focus (ink, label "Add to this stage"), open (menu). **Keyboard:** reachable in tab order between steps; Return opens; arrow keys move in the menu; Esc closes and returns focus. **Accessibility:** `aria-label="Add to Stage 2 after 2.3: prompt or inspection item, this project only"`, `aria-haspopup="menu"`.

## Lock (F-004)

A 12 px lock glyph after the state on every core step, gate, packet and lessons item. Tooltip and accessible text: "Core: protects production quality. Change it in the blueprint with a Change Request." The selected core step repeats this line under its body. Core steps never show Skip. Locked stages (strip, sidebar) use the same glyph with "Locked until Stage 2 is signed".

## Not-applicable row (F-004)

Hatched background (`--hatch` 1 px lines at 135°, 7 px apart), dashed callout, one line: "Not applicable: TradeLog isn't sold, so there's no market to tear down (commercial = no)". No actions. If a condition later changes (before the Stage 2 sign, or via a CR in v1.1) the row returns to not started. **Accessibility:** the reason is text (`--ink` on hatch passes 4.5:1); the row isn't focusable unless it has a link to the condition.

## Inspector (F-030, P2)

Contextual right column with a segmented tab list: Packet, Gate, Lessons. Shows the selected step's packet, the stage's inspection items with kinds and what each waits for, lessons, or evidence for a selected gate item. Empty states invite the next action ("No lessons for Stage 2 yet. F.2 writes them…"). **Keyboard:** ⌘⌥I toggles; ← → move between tabs. **Accessibility:** `<aside aria-label="Inspector">`, `role="tablist"`/`tab`/`tabpanel`. Expanding animates width over 140 ms when motion is allowed.

## Packet view (F-013)

Header line in mono, then the packet's fields as a readable two-column list. A summary bar states the count ("2 problems") and what Sign waits for. Each problem is inline on its field: fail wash, 3 px fail bar, the message ("OPEN ITEMS must be exactly "none". Resolve this item, or move it to KNOWN LIMITS with a ticket ID.") and its fix actions: **Repair** (P2, re-arms the stage's chain with "Fix only these problems…"), **Open in editor**, or **Copy fix request** for chat packets. Normalizations show as notes (accent bar), not errors. The inspector shows the raw file with problem lines marked, and on Stage 2 the **Confirm conditions** box (undecided blocks signing).

**Accessibility.** `<dl aria-label="Packet fields">`; the summary is `role="status"` and updates when the watcher re-validates. The raw view is a focusable scroll region.

## Inspection row and attest control (F-015, P2)

**Inspection row:** a drafting stamp in the margin (FAIL in `--fail`, PASS in `--pass`, "Waiting" dashed and muted), the item text, its kind and how to check, the evidence (imported or typed), and artifacts with copy buttons for paths and commands. Review order: FAIL first, then waiting items, then PASS collapsed into one disclosure. After a FAIL: **Fix this** opens a new CLI chain (Opus 5.5, high, Plan); the third failure offers the F.4 prompt.

**Attest control:** a segmented Done / Not applicable choice, then a note (Done: at least 15 characters, not repeated within the gate, optional linked file or URL) or a reason (Not applicable). Character count under the field. "Confirm Done" records it. Imported results are proposals until confirmed. A changed item text shows "Re-confirm kind" and can't be attested until confirmed.

**Close-out sequence:** Gate A → Results → Gate B packet → Gate C lessons → Sign, as a one-line ordered list with the current step marked. **Sign packet** stays disabled with the reason ("Sign is locked: 1 item failed and 2 wait for your note. Then Gate B and Gate C.").

**Sign dialog (F-014):** name and date (prefilled), branch (current branch, default; or another branch), "Fields Foreman will add" as a diff (APPROVED BY, SKIPPED, SUBSTITUTIONS, ATTESTED), the content hash, the exact paths committed with hooks off, the commit message, and Cancel / **Sign packet**. Stage 2 adds Confirm conditions. This sheet is the **review**; **Sign packet** only requests signing, and Rust then raises the native macOS confirmation (name, date, "Signing stage-05.md @ a3f9c1", conditions, chain state; SR-24a). Cancel in the native dialog leaves everything as it was. Toast after read-back: "Packet signed. Committed 4e1b2c7 on stage/05-site-prep and read back 3 files." Then the stamp.

**Accessibility.** Items are a list; stamps are `role="img"` with "Failed", "Passed", "Waiting for you". The attest choice is a `radiogroup`; note errors use `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`. The dialog is `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`, labelled by its title and described by the hash line; focus starts on Name, is trapped inside, Esc cancels and returns focus to Sign packet.

## Terminal tab and drawer (F-009)

**Drawer:** bottom panel with a grip (`role="separator"`, ↑ ↓ resize by 16 px), a tab bar, the Now/Next strip, and xterm. Collapsed state is a single bar ("Terminal: no sessions in TradeLog. Chat steps run in claude.ai."). Slides 160 ms when motion is allowed.

**Tab:** named after the session in mono (`fm-tradelog-s02-2-3`), with a state glyph and word: running (dot, accent), idle (P2, ring), blocked (P2, warn in attention: "waiting for permission"), exited 0 (check), exited non-zero / errored (cross, fail), interrupted (broken link). Tab menu: Interrupt (Ctrl-C), Terminate (asks first when not idle), Copy session ID, Resume. Closing a running tab asks first and says the session ends. After a re-arm the previous buffer is an archived, collapsed, read-only section at the top ("Earlier in this session: 2.2 Convene the council, 1,842 lines").

**Keyboard.** ⌃` toggles the drawer; with the terminal focused every key goes to Claude Code except ⌘-chords, ⌃`, ⌘C/⌘V/⌘A/⌘F; ⌘⇧E returns focus to the workspace (never a trap); ⌘⇧T focus mode (P2); ⌘K opens the palette from the terminal.

**Accessibility.** Tabs are a `tablist`; the panel is a focusable `tabpanel` named after the session. With Settings → Screen reader mode on, launches add `--ax-screen-reader` and xterm's screen-reader mode is on (Foreman doesn't detect VoiceOver itself). The terminal keeps the theme (`--term-*`), which passes 4.5:1 for ink, muted text and status colors.

## Now/Next strip (F-009, P2)

A one-line band above the terminal: **Now** live mode, model and effort from the sensor ("Accept edits, Fable 5.1, high"); **Next** the in-step cue or the next step ("2.4 Stack decision re-arms this session when Claude is idle"); at the right, the round tracker for steps with several outputs ("r6-feature-specs.md written"). **States:** match; mismatch (mode guard: the Now value turns `--attention` with "Plan expected"); no sensor (Next only). **Accessibility:** `role="status" aria-live="polite"`, so a mode change is announced once, never on every token.

## Config-changed banner (F-008)

Full-width band under the stage header, attention bar and wash: "Claude Code configuration in spec/ changed. principal-engineer.md was edited at 13:52. Review it before 2.4 launches." Actions: Review diff, Acknowledge (requests the native acknowledgement dialog Rust raises, naming the changed sources; SR-24a). A blocking variant (fail bar and wash) names the file and key for auth-changing settings: "apiKeyHelper in spec/.claude/settings.json would move billing off your plan. Remove it, then check again." **Accessibility:** `role="status"`; the blocking variant is `role="alert"`. Acknowledge moves focus back to the step.

## Outside-change review (F-022)

A full review screen per pending change: what changed and when (and which session was running), whether git has a commit for it, the approved hash; then one diff per decision file with a pill for its effect ("Changes the path", "Has no effect") and one line per consequence ("2.5 is core; Foreman ignores this skip"). Variables reload on their own with a toast. Sticky footer: **Reject and restore** (atomic write of the approved content) and **Accept changes** (requests the native dialog "Accept this change to <file>? It changes: <decision keys>"; nothing is accepted until Billy answers it, SR-24a). **Keyboard:** Tab reaches each diff (scroll regions are focusable), then the footer; ⌘↵ accepts, Esc leaves without deciding (the change stays pending in Today). **Accessibility:** diffs mark lines with + and − characters as well as color.

## Build-package assembler (F-023, P2)

A panel on Stage 4's workspace after the 4.8 packet is valid (`assembler_view`; mockup `assembler.html`). Three parts, top to bottom:

1. **REPO FILES** from the Stage 4 packet, one row per path in packet order: a state glyph and word (**exists** check/pass, **missing** ring/muted, **empty** warn/attention, **refused** cross/fail with the path-policy rule, e.g. "Refused: `.github/` isn't writable by Foreman"), the path in mono, and actions **Paste into** (clipboard → `assembler_paste_preview`, then a diff sheet with Create or Overwrite and **Write file**) and **Reveal**. Root `CLAUDE.md` has a lock-style note "Claude Code loads this file in every session"; its Write requests the native confirmation Rust raises (SR-24a, AC-F021-06). A summary line counts rows ("8 of 12 files ready; 2 missing, 1 empty, 1 refused").
2. **Late conditions** (shown while any is `undecided` and Stage 4 isn't signed): one tri-state row each for `has_database`, `has_uploads`, `has_email`, `has_integrations`. A proposal from path evidence (DR-063) shows as a pre-selected but **unconfirmed** choice with "from: `docs/migrations/0001_init.sql`" and a **Confirm** button; a condition with no evidence stays Undecided with "No evidence in REPO FILES or the repo; decide it yourself". Confirming records the condition with the note "from evidence: <path>".
3. **Package complete** attest item: offered only when every row that Foreman may write exists and isn't empty (AC-F023-05); otherwise it says which rows block it. A refused row doesn't block it (Foreman can never write it; Billy creates it himself).

**States:** no Stage 4 packet yet (empty state pointing to 4.8), loading, all ready, rows missing, a refused row, read-only project. **Keyboard:** rows are a list; Tab reaches each row's actions; the late-conditions controls are `radiogroup`s (← →). **Accessibility:** each row's state is text, not color only; the summary is `role="status"`; the diff sheet is `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"` and returns focus to the row's Paste into.

## Toast

Bottom-right, `--sheet-raised` with the overlay shadow, an icon and one sentence that repeats the action's verb ("Packet signed…", "Variables reloaded…"). Stays 6 s, longer while hovered or focused; never holds the only copy of important information. **Accessibility:** one shared `role="status" aria-live="polite"` region; errors that need action are banners, not toasts.

## Command palette (F-028, P2)

⌘K opens a 640 px dialog over a scrim: a search field, results grouped as Steps, Actions, Projects, Stages, each row with an icon, a title, a muted context ("TradeLog, Stage 2, waits for 2.3"), and a shortcut hint. Footer: ↑↓ move, ↵ open, ⌘↵ run, ⌘G jump to a stage. No matches: "No steps or actions match "stak". Try a step number like 2.4." **Keyboard:** typing filters; ↑ ↓ move; Return opens; ⌘↵ runs the step; Esc closes and returns focus where it was. **Accessibility:** `role="dialog" aria-modal`, the input is a `combobox` with `aria-activedescendant` into a `listbox` of `option`s grouped with `role="group"`; the background is `inert`.

## Setup form fields (F-018)

Single-column form (P1) with a sticky "What Foreman will write" panel; the P2 wizard splits it into four steps with the same fields and ends on "Review your path" (stages that apply and don't, and why; the strip preview; the file writes).

- **Name, Folder** (with Choose…). Folder errors: inside another project, the blueprint repo, not writable, newer `.foreman/` schema (opens read-only).
- **claude.ai Project link.**
- **Variables** with their bracket token shown in mono. A value equal to the blueprint placeholder is an error: "This is still the blueprint's placeholder. Describe who it's for in your words." Defaults from Settings say so.
- **Conditions:** each a tri-state segmented control (Yes / No / Undecided, `role="radiogroup"`) with one line on what it changes; the code ID in mono for the audit trail.
- **What Foreman will write:** Create, Leave alone, Refuse to overwrite, each path with its reason. **Create project** stays disabled with the fields to fix named.

**Keyboard:** Tab order follows the visual order; ← → move inside a segmented control; ⌘↵ submits when valid. **Accessibility:** every field has a `<label>`; errors use `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`; the write list is a description list so VoiceOver reads "Blueprint, 2.2 at a3f9c1".

## Doctor and first run (F-019)

Check rows: glyph, name, detail (what was found and the fix in plain words), found value. Failing rows get the fail wash and bar. Capability table: feature, minimum, found, the steps it blocks. Contract probes (P2): pass, flag failure, inconclusive (keeps the last verified state). First run is a vertical checklist (Blueprint repo → Template health → Your name → Doctor with the probe-folder trust step → Start your first project) whose last step is the empty state for a new install.

## Focus, motion and theming notes

- Focus ring: 2 px `--focus` with 2 px offset everywhere (`--linework` in Night, `--cyanotype` in Vellum, `--focus-on-strip` on the strip). Never removed; `:focus-visible` only hides it for pointer clicks.
- Reduce Motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`): all durations become 0 and the stamp doesn't animate. Reduce Transparency (`prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`): the sidebar uses solid `--sidebar`, and the webview reports it to Rust, which turns the window's vibrancy off. Increase Contrast (`prefers-contrast: more`): the high-contrast token set applies, with every text/background pair ≥ 7:1 (AC-XC-23; built in M5-04: `--rule` and `--ink-muted` step up, the status, accent, strip, terminal and disabled-control colors are darker in Vellum and lighter in Night, and `contrast-check.py` checks every text pair at 7:1 with the set on).
- Themes follow the system unless Settings picks Night or Vellum (`data-theme` on the root).
- Fonts are local only. The drafting face (IBM Plex Sans Condensed SemiBold, OFL) ships in the bundle; mockups fall back to a local condensed face.

## Mockup fixture notes

- TradeLog's conditions in the mockups: `idea_known` yes, `commercial` no (a free tool for a trading community), `payments` undecided, the rest yes. That makes Stage 0, 1.3 and 2.1 not applicable and Stage 10 undecided. `gate.html` is a later snapshot (Stage 5, payments decided no).
- Blueprint 2.2 annotates Stages 0–5, so 6–18 show as needing annotation (PROPOSAL.md B-1).
- `x-2-1` in `workspace.html` is a project-only step (F-025) used to show a run-input form and an optional part inside Stage 2, which has neither in the blueprint.
- `gate.html#sign` shows the sign dialog and `gate.html#signed` shows the stamp and the toast (CSS `:target`, no script).
